# Product

## Register

Brand for the public profile site. Product for owner-only workspaces such as the job application manager.

## Platform

web

Responsive Next.js application deployed on Vercel.

## Users

- Primary public audience: recruiters, hiring managers, and collaborators evaluating Kim Daejeong's work.
- Primary workspace user: Kim Daejeong, preparing and tracking job applications.

## Product Purpose

Make verified engineering evidence easy to inspect publicly, and turn that same evidence into practical private workflows for selecting roles and preparing tailored applications.

## Positioning

An evidence-first engineering profile: candid about scope and gaps, specific about systems built, and optimized for careful technical evaluation rather than promotional spectacle.

## Conversion and Proof

Public pages should lead to a resume, portfolio evidence, or a direct conversation. Workspace pages should lead to a concrete next action such as verifying a posting, tailoring a resume, or submitting an application. Proof comes from stable claim IDs and verified project evidence.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, technical, direct, curious, and quietly confident. Dense information should remain calm and legible.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards with decorative metrics and interchangeable cards.
- Flashy portfolio treatments that obscure evidence.
- Inflated career claims or implied experience that cannot be verified.
- Interfaces that blur public and private information boundaries.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before persuasion.
- Decisions and next actions before decoration.
- Use the existing monochrome system; reserve color for semantic status.
- Prefer compact master-detail workflows over card grids for operational screens.
- Keep provenance, verification date, and uncertainty visible.

The visual hierarchy, typography, divider, list, responsive, print, and visual QA
contracts are maintained in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve visible keyboard focus, avoid color-only status communication, support reduced motion, and keep the workspace usable from mobile through wide desktop layouts.
